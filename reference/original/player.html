MIDI map, keyboard, and what the model does
Note on / offfinger on the bowl (velocity → finger load) / finger lifts. Bars: mallet strike (velocity → speed); note off does nothing unless brake is held.

Program 0 / 1glass / bars

Each voice is the same model as the design simulation: two degenerate (2,0) modes plus the (3,0) pair on a 20 g modal mass, stick-slip friction from a wet finger, Coriolis (Bryan) coupling when the bowl turns, regenerative coil drive with an amplitude regulator, braking against the full velocity vector, an electronic spring for vibrato and pitch bend, anisotropic trim, and stereo radiation sampled at two ear angles. Bars are a fundamental plus an arched 4× partial with a mallet force pulse and the same coil laws. Eight voices; the quietest is stolen. Web MIDI needs Chrome or Edge on a desktop; Safari has no MIDI, but keys and QWERTY work everywhere.
